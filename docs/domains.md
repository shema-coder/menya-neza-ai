# MENYA NEZA AI — Domain Definitions

## 1. Agriculture

Potential indicators include:

- Crop production
- Crop yield
- Cultivated area
- Agricultural losses
- Seasonal production
- Historical agricultural performance
- Rainfall-related agricultural indicators
- Drought-related agricultural indicators
- Weather impacts on agriculture

The system should identify unusual agricultural patterns and changes
from relevant historical baselines.

## 2. Food

Potential indicators include:

- Food production
- Food availability
- Food supply
- Food prices where reliable data is available
- Historical food trends
- Agricultural losses affecting food availability
- Population-related indicators
- Food-security-related indicators

The system should prefer terms such as "Food Pressure Signal" when
evidence indicates elevated pressure but does not justify a stronger
claim such as "food crisis."

## 3. Disasters

Potential indicators include:

- Flood events
- Drought events
- Landslides
- Extreme rainfall
- Disaster-related agricultural damage
- Affected areas
- Affected populations
- Historical disaster events
- Weather-related hazard indicators

The system should communicate disaster-related risk indicators and
unusual patterns.

It must not claim certainty without sufficient evidence.

Good example:

"Elevated flood-related signal detected."

Avoid:

"A flood will definitely happen tomorrow."

## 4. Core Relationship

DISASTER → AGRICULTURE → FOOD → PEOPLE

This relationship helps the platform examine how environmental and
disaster-related conditions may affect agriculture, food availability,
and ultimately communities.

The relationship does not imply that every disaster automatically
causes agricultural or food problems.
