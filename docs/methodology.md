# MENYA NEZA AI — Methodology

## 1. Purpose

MENYA NEZA AI aims to transform relevant Rwanda data into understandable
early-warning signals.

## 2. Focus Areas

The initial system focuses on:

1. Agriculture
2. Food
3. Disasters

## 3. Analytical Pipeline

RAW DATA
↓
CLEAN AND STANDARDIZE
↓
ANALYZE
↓
DETECT UNUSUAL PATTERNS
↓
CALCULATE RISK / SIGNAL
↓
GENERATE WARNING
↓
EXPLAIN WHY
↓
DISPLAY FOR USERS
↓
SUPPORT EARLY ACTION

## 4. Warning Principle

The system communicates risk indicators and unusual patterns.

It must not claim that a disaster will definitely happen.

Good example:

"Elevated flood-related signal detected."

Avoid:

"A flood will happen tomorrow."

## 5. Initial Warning Levels

NORMAL: 0–39

WATCH: 40–69

HIGH CONCERN: 70–100

These thresholds are provisional.

They must later be tested using historical data, model evaluation,
backtesting, false-positive analysis, and false-negative analysis.

## 6. Explainability

Every important warning should answer:

- What changed?
- Where did it change?
- Compared with what normal baseline?
- Why does it matter?
- What evidence supports the signal?
- How confident is the system?
- What period was analyzed?

## 7. Responsible AI

The platform should disclose:

- Data sources
- Data limitations
- Model limitations
- Confidence
- Historical validation
- Model version
- Analysis period

AI-generated explanations must be based on verified analytical results
and must not invent evidence.

## 8. Data Integrity

MENYA NEZA AI must not present synthetic, fabricated, or unverified
information as actual Rwanda observations.

Every dataset used for analysis must have documented source information,
geographic coverage, time period, variables, and known limitations.
